Difficulty (1 Player)

Beginner

Slow ball. AI reacts only when the ball moves toward it and tracks the center of the ball — easy to beat with angled shots.

Medium

Normal speed. AI is quicker and has a smaller dead-zone. Decent rallies, fair challenge.

Expert

Fast ball. AI predicts where the ball will land using wall-bounce physics and deliberately aims for the edges of its paddle to send hard-angle shots at your corners. Always repositions, even when the ball is moving away.

Tips

Hit the ball with the edge of your paddle to change its angle. The further from center, the steeper the shot.
